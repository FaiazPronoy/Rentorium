How Rentorium works

{% icon "shield-check" %}

Every listing is checked

Someone on our team reads the details and looks at the ownership paper before a property is published. Nothing goes live automatically.

{% icon "calendar" %}

Viewings are booked online

Pick a date and time, and the owner confirms it. The same slot cannot be booked twice.

{% icon "lock" %}

Private papers stay private

Ownership documents are visible only to the owner and to a verified member of our team. They are never served from a public address.

{% icon "star" %}

Fair reviews

Each person can review a property once, and owners cannot review their own listings.

Who we build for

Renters and buyers looking for a place, owners who want to list a property, and agents who check listings before they go live.
