What we do differently

✅ Every listing is checked An agent reads the details and looks at the ownership paper before anything goes live.
📅 Visits are booked, not negotiated Pick a slot, the owner confirms, and the system makes double booking impossible.
🔒 Private papers stay private Ownership documents are visible only to the owner and a verified agent. Never on the public page.
⭐ Real reviews One review per person per property, and an owner cannot review their own listing.

Who this is for

Renters who want to see a real price and a real photo. Owners who want their place in front of people who are actually looking. Agents who need one screen to keep the whole thing honest.
