Privacy and legal

How we handle your information, and who we are.

What we collect

Your name, email address and phone number, so owners know who they are speaking to. If you list a property we also keep the details you enter, the photographs you upload and, where you provide it, the ownership document.

Who can see it

Your name, profile photograph and the properties you have listed are public. Your phone number is shared with the other party only after a viewing has been accepted. Ownership documents are shown to you and to a verified member of our team, and to nobody else.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in and remembers the properties you have added to a comparison. We do not run advertising or third party tracking.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from your profile page at any time. Your listings, messages, bookings and documents are removed with it.
