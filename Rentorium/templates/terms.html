Terms of use

Last updated {% now "d F Y" %}. This is a student project, not a real estate agency, and nothing here is legal advice.

1. Using the site

You need an account to list a property, book a visit or message an owner. Keep your password to yourself and tell us if you think someone else has it.

2. Listing a property

List only what you own or are allowed to let. The details and the photos have to be the real ones. An agent reviews every listing and can send it back or take it down.

3. Visits

A booking is a request until the owner accepts it. Turning up without an accepted booking is between you and the owner.

4. Reviews

One review per person per property. Write about the place and the experience, not about the person. Reviews that are abusive get removed.

5. Your data

We keep what you give us so the site can work. Ownership documents are shown only to you and to a verified agent. Delete your account and everything on it goes with it.

6. What we do not do

We do not hold deposits, act as a broker, or guarantee any listing. Check the property and the paperwork yourself before money changes hands.
