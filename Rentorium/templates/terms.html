Terms of use

Last updated {% now "d F Y" %}. These terms cover how you may use {{ SITE_NAME }}. They are written plainly on purpose; they are not legal advice.

1. Using the site

You need an account to list a property, book a viewing or message an owner. Keep your password to yourself and tell us straight away if you think somebody else has it.

2. Listing a property

List only what you own or are authorised to let. The details and the photographs must be the real ones. Our team reviews every listing and can send it back for changes or take it down.

3. Viewings

A booking is a request until the owner accepts it. Turning up without an accepted booking is a matter between you and the owner.

4. Reviews

One review per person per property. Write about the place and the experience, not about the person. Abusive reviews are removed.

5. Your data

We keep what you give us so the service can work. Ownership documents are shown only to you and to a verified member of our team. Delete your account and everything attached to it goes with it.

6. What we do not do

We do not hold deposits, act as a broker, or guarantee any listing. Inspect the property and the paperwork yourself before money changes hands.

7. Changes

If these terms change in a way that affects you, we will say so on this page and date the change at the top.
